# Country coverage

This document separates catalog coverage from teaching readiness. A subject name in a syllabus proves catalog coverage only. It does not claim complete topics, assessments, examples, or educator approval.

## Completion levels

1. **Subject catalog** — every in-scope official subject has metadata.
2. **Standards catalog** — every official grade or phase has ordered topics and learning outcomes.
3. **Teachable content** — each topic reaches `quality_level: 3` with teaching notes, deterministic assessment, and worked examples.
4. **Reviewed content** — qualified educators approve the curriculum mapping and pedagogy.

## Malaysia

### KSSR Semakan 2017

The repository includes all 20 Tahap Dua subjects listed in the Ministry of Education's 2025 school-based assessment guide. This includes the seven subjects with ministry-provided formats and the thirteen subjects with school-provided formats.

Current status: complete Tahap Dua subject catalog. Grade-level topics and teaching artifacts remain to be authored from the matching DSKP documents.

### KSSM

The repository includes all 23 menengah rendah subjects listed in the same 2025 guide. Mathematics, Science, and History already contain Forms 1–5 topic inventories. The other subjects are catalog metadata until their DSKP-aligned grade content is added.

Current authored topic baseline:

- Mathematics: 53 canonical topics
- Science: 53 canonical topics
- History: 46 canonical topics

Menengah atas has many elective, religious, arts, technical, and vocational packages. Those subjects require a separate catalog because availability depends on school type and package. Do not describe the current KSSM catalog as complete for every upper-secondary package.

## Indonesia

### Kurikulum Merdeka

The repository includes the complete 46-subject general and non-vocational catalog from the national learning-outcome annex. The six Agama dan Budi Pekerti entries use the 2026 amendment as their catalog authority. Their phase content remains pending ingestion from the amended learning outcomes.

Matematika has a complete Fase A-F standards catalog with 28 ordered element topics. Each topic is `quality_level: 1`; teaching notes, examples, assessments, and educator review remain pending.

Current status: complete general subject catalog and complete Matematika phase standards. The vocational catalog and non-Mathematics phase standards remain pending.

Indonesia continued to permit both Kurikulum Merdeka and Kurikulum 2013 for the 2025/2026 school year. They must remain separate syllabus families in OSS.
